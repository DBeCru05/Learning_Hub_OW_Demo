Synaptic Learning Builder

You tell it what you want to learn. It builds a full, personalized learning experience — just for you. No instructional design background needed. No waiting for L&D to build it. Just curiosity and a topic you want to master.

This is a beta experience. Content is AI-generated and may not replace formal training programs.
🚧
Synaptic Learning Builder is under active development
We're building a tool that lets any employee create their own AI-powered learning experience from scratch. What you see here is the working beta — real AI generation, real content, real XP. As the platform matures, paths you complete will be added to the shared Synaptic library for others to discover.
🔬 Beta · Open to all staff
How it works
1
💡
You pick a topic
Tell the builder what you want to learn — a skill, concept, tool, or behavior. Anything relevant to your role or growth.
2
🤖
AI builds your path
The builder generates a structured learning journey — context, key concepts, scenarios, and checkpoints — calibrated to your role and time.
3
🎮
You learn and play
Work through your path at your own pace. Earn XP, hit milestones, and track your progress — including saving mid-course for longer paths.
4
📚
Share with others
Once complete, your path can be contributed to the Synaptic learning library for colleagues to discover and take.
Built on three principles
Agency
Learning works best when you choose it. The builder starts with what you want to learn, not what someone decided you should.
Quality without gatekeeping
AI ensures every path meets a baseline before it reaches others — so the library stays useful without creating bottlenecks for L&D.
Learning as contribution
When you build and share a path, you're not just growing yourself — you're adding to your organization's collective intelligence.
What you'll get
🎯
Self-directed learning
You own your path from start to finish. No one prescribes what you learn — you do.
⚡
Built in minutes
No instructional design experience needed. The builder handles the structure — you bring the topic.
🏆
Gamified progress
XP, milestones, and completion badges keep you moving and make learning feel worth finishing.
💬
Conversational learning
The AI challenges your thinking and adapts to how you're doing — not just delivers content at you.
Based on
📖
Learning science + generative AI
Synaptic Learning Builder is grounded in evidence-based instructional design principles — spaced repetition, retrieval practice, scenario-based learning, and self-determination theory — combined with the generative capabilities of modern AI. The result is a learning experience that adapts to you, not the other way around.
