Synaptic Learning Builder

You tell it what you want to learn. It builds a full, personalized learning experience just for you. No instructional design background needed. No waiting for L&D to build it.

This is a beta experience. Content is AI-generated and may not replace formal training programs.

Synaptic Learning Builder is under active development
We are building a tool that lets any employee create their own AI-powered learning experience from scratch. What you see here is the working beta — real content, real XP. As the platform matures, paths you complete will be added to the shared Synaptic library for others to discover.
